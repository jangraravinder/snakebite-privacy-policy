## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **SnakeBite** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user data, advertising identifiers, and device information when you download, install, and play SnakeBite.

By using SnakeBite, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Local Game Data (Stored on Device Only)

SnakeBite saves the following data **locally on your device** using Android SharedPreferences. This data is **never transmitted to our servers**:

- **Player Display Name** — Your chosen in-game username.
- **Snake Skin Selection** — Your currently selected and unlocked snake customisations.
- **Game Progress & High Score** — Your personal best score and level progression.
- **App Settings** — Music, sound effects, vibration, and sensitivity preferences.

### B. Advertising Data (Collected by Google AdMob)

SnakeBite uses **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to display banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

- **Advertising Identifier (AAID)** — Android Advertising ID for personalised ad delivery.
- **Device Information** — Device model, Android version, hardware specs, screen resolution.
- **Network & IP Address** — Used for coarse location-based ad targeting and fraud prevention.
- **Ad Interaction Data** — Impressions, clicks, rewarded video completions, and ad performance metrics.

This data is processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy.

## 5. Permissions Used

SnakeBite requests the following Android permissions:

| Permission | Reason |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | Required for displaying AdMob advertisements |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Allows AdMob to check connectivity before loading ads |
| `VIBRATE` | Optional in-game haptic feedback on snake death and collisions |

We do **not** request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Local game data** (scores, settings, player name) remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
- **AdMob data** is retained and managed by Google LLC according to their own data retention policies.

---

## 8. Your Choices & Opt-Out Options

### Advertising Opt-Out
You can limit personalised advertising on your Android device:
1. Open **Settings** on your Android device.
2. Navigate to **Privacy → Ads**.
3. Tap **Delete Advertising ID** or enable **Opt out of Ads Personalisation**.

### In-App Controls
SnakeBite provides a built-in **Settings screen** allowing you to:
- Toggle **Background Music** on/off
- Toggle **Sound Effects** on/off
- Toggle **Vibration** on/off

---

## 9. Data Security

- All local game data is stored within Android's secure sandboxed app storage.
- AdMob communications are encrypted in transit via HTTPS using Google's infrastructure.
- The app does not implement its own backend database or cloud storage.
